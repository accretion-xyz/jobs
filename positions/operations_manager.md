# Operations Manager - Solana Security

- **Company:** Accretion Labs
- **Location:** Remote
- **Type:** Full-time

## About Accretion Labs

We audit Solana programs and research vulnerabilities in the Solana ecosystem. We're looking for someone to own the business operations that support the audit team and our clients.

## The Role

You'll own the day-to-day administration and processes that keep engagements running: contracts, invoicing, payments, client onboarding, vendor coordination, and team administration.

We need an experienced operator who has already owned these workflows and can run them independently. You should be comfortable spotting missing information, following up, resolving routine issues, and escalating decisions that need the team's input.

Success means engagements move through onboarding, billing, and closeout reliably, records stay current, and the audit team spends less time chasing administrative work.

## What You'll Own

- Prepare and track client and contractor agreements using approved templates; coordinate changes with the team and legal advisers when needed.
- Issue invoices, track receivables, follow up on overdue payments, and coordinate contractor payments and expenses through agreed approval processes.
- Maintain accurate financial and engagement records and coordinate with external accounting, tax, and legal advisers.
- Run client onboarding and routine engagement communications, coordinating handoffs with business development and the audit team.
- Manage vendors, contractor administration, team logistics, and hiring and onboarding administration.
- Improve templates, workflows, and systems; automate recurring work where it saves time and reduces errors.
- Keep the team informed about outstanding obligations, deadlines, and issues that require a decision.

## What You Need to Demonstrate

- **Relevant operations ownership.** Typically three or more years in business operations at a small company, consultancy, or professional services firm, or equivalent evidence of running these workflows independently.
- **Practical financial administration.** Experience with invoicing, receivables, contractor payments, expense tracking, and reliable records.
- **Process improvement.** Examples of removing an operational bottleneck or reducing recurring work, with a clear explanation of the result.
- **Clear communication and follow-through.** You can manage several engagements, keep clients and colleagues informed, and close outstanding tasks without repeated reminders.
- **Sound judgment.** You handle sensitive business information carefully and know when financial, contractual, or specialist questions need approval or professional advice.

Crypto or security industry experience is useful. You don't need to audit code. Experience with remote teams and tools such as GitHub, a CRM, and accounting software is also useful; the ability to run reliable processes matters more than a particular tool.

Email careers@accretion.xyz with the subject **Operations Manager - [Your Name]** and include:

1. **Your CV or profile**, highlighting the business operations you've personally owned.
2. **One or two concrete examples of your work.** Describe the starting problem, what you changed, and the result. Include at least one example relevant to client engagements, billing, contracts, or contractor administration.
