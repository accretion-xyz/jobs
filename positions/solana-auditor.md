# Senior Solana Security Auditor

- **Company:** Accretion Labs
- **Location:** Remote

## About Accretion Labs

We audit Solana programs and research vulnerabilities in the Solana ecosystem. Our team has been finding bugs in Solana since 2022. We work directly with protocol teams to understand their systems, find vulnerabilities, and help them ship fixes.

## The Role

We're hiring selectively for an auditor who can independently review complex production Solana programs and contribute to client audits from the start.

**This is a senior role. We don't currently have capacity to hire or train junior auditors, beginners, or people looking for their first audit experience.**

We care more about demonstrated ability than job titles or years of experience. If you have an exceptional security research track record without a conventional auditing background, we'd still like to hear from you. You need to meet the same practical bar: understand an unfamiliar Solana codebase, identify meaningful vulnerabilities, validate exploitability, and explain your findings clearly without training in the fundamentals.

## What You'll Own

- Build an understanding of a protocol's architecture, trust assumptions, account relationships, and economic invariants.
- Review Rust, Anchor, and native Solana code for vulnerabilities, including issues specific to the protocol's design.
- Develop reproducible tests or proofs of concept and assess impact under realistic conditions.
- Write precise reports that explain the root cause, exploit conditions, impact, and recommended fix.
- Discuss findings with clients, review fixes, and communicate uncertainty or scope limitations early.
- Collaborate with other auditors, review their findings, and improve our research methods and tooling.

## What You Need to Demonstrate

- **A strong security track record.** Substantive findings from audits, security research, or bug bounties, with a clear explanation of what you personally discovered. Contest findings can count when they demonstrate the same depth of work.
- **Independent audit ability.** You can take an unfamiliar, nontrivial codebase from initial understanding through validated findings and a clear report without close supervision.
- **Strong Rust and Solana expertise.** You can reason about account ownership and validation, signers and authorities, PDAs, CPIs, token behavior, and instruction and state transitions in both Anchor and native programs.
- **Protocol reasoning.** You can identify broken invariants and flaws in business logic, permissions, accounting, and economic assumptions beyond a checklist of common bugs.
- **Careful validation and communication.** You distinguish exploitable issues from intended behavior, justify severity, and explain the conditions required for an attack.

Course certificates, tutorial projects, CTF participation, contest rankings, or familiarity with common vulnerabilities alone don't demonstrate readiness for this role. Show us the underlying work.

## Useful Additional Experience

- Reviewing complex DeFi protocols, such as lending markets, exchanges, or vaults.
- Building security tools, fuzzing harnesses, or tests for protocol invariants.
- Working with Token-2022, low-level SVM behavior, cryptography, or other relevant specialist areas.
- Publishing clear technical research or contributing to Solana security tooling.

## Working With Us

- Remote work with direct collaboration across the audit team and client engineers.
- Hands-on research into production Solana systems.
- Room to improve how we audit and build tools that make the work more effective.

## How to Apply

Email careers@accretion.xyz with the subject **Senior Solana Security Auditor - [Your Name]** and include:

1. **A short summary of your relevant experience**, with a CV or profile link if useful.
2. **Two or three of your strongest findings or research examples.** For each, link the report, write-up, or proof of concept where available and explain the root cause, impact, and your personal contribution. Include substantive Solana work so we can assess your readiness for this role.
3. **An example of work you owned independently.** Explain the scope and how you approached understanding the system, investigating issues, and validating findings. This can be one of the examples above.
4. **Your timezone, availability, and preferred working arrangement.**

Existing work is enough for the initial application; you don't need to perform a new audit to apply. If your strongest work is confidential, describe what you can share and offer a sanitized example or a reference who can discuss your contribution. Don't disclose client-confidential material.

We'll contact you if your experience and evidence look like a fit.
