# Marketing & Content Lead - Solana Security

- **Company:** Accretion Labs
- **Location:** Remote
- **Type:** Full-time or part-time

## About Accretion Labs

We audit Solana programs and research vulnerabilities in the Solana ecosystem. Our content should help developers understand security problems and help protocol teams understand our work.

## The Role

We're looking for an experienced writer and content owner who can turn technical research into clear, accurate material and manage publication from idea to distribution.

You should already have published work and experience owning a content program. You'll work with auditors for technical input and review, while independently handling research, drafting, editing, and distribution. This role requires more than scheduling social posts or following a brief.

For a part-time arrangement, we'll agree on a focused scope and publishing cadence that fit the available time.

## What You'll Own

- Set content priorities around our research, services, and the questions Solana developers and protocol teams actually have.
- Turn audit findings and research into technical articles, case studies, explainers, and social posts with input from auditors.
- Manage the editorial calendar, gather the necessary technical input, and take drafts through review and publication.
- Distribute content through relevant channels and improve website copy and discoverability.
- Assess what reaches the right audience and leads to useful conversations, using evidence beyond follower counts.
- Coordinate supporting material for talks, conferences, and other opportunities when relevant to the agreed scope.
- Get technical review and client permission before publishing material from client work.

## What You Need to Demonstrate

- **Strong published writing.** You can explain a complex technical topic clearly while preserving the details that matter.
- **Content ownership.** You've managed work from idea through publication and distribution with limited supervision.
- **Relevant audience judgment.** You understand how to reach developers and protocol teams, with examples from crypto, security, or another technical field.
- **Research and editorial judgment.** You can read technical source material, ask useful questions, verify claims, and recognize when you need an auditor's review.
- **Evidence of results.** You can explain the purpose of previous work, your contribution, and what it achieved.

Solana or security experience is useful. A large personal following is optional; the quality of your work and your ability to own the role matter more.

## Compensation

We'll discuss compensation and benefits based on experience, scope, and whether the arrangement is full-time or part-time.
